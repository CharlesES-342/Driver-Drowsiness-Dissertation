\chapter*{Conclusion}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion}
\stepcounter{chapter}

The final outcome of this project was the development of nine models, each incorporating subtle variations. These models explored both factual,
state‑based classifications and opinion‑driven classifications, where my own judgement was used as a basis for tiredness deduction. This approach
introduced challenges during testing, as simulated tiredness behaves differently from genuine tiredness, making it more difficult to create reliable
test scenarios throughout development. Despite these limitations, the system achieved an overall accuracy of 77.34\%, with a built‑in safety bias
that favoured misclassifying Alert states as Tired rather than the reverse. This is the ideal given this light-weight model, being able to acheive a
fast refresh-rate (around 5fps),

All of this was integrated into a simple, intuitive interface that can be accessed remotely through a convenient web‑based platform. The system
operates as a headless display, allowing the hardware to remain hidden and unobtrusive while still providing real‑time feedback whenever it is needed.
